Crime Report.AI uses a configurable "authority" layer so that reports can be
routed to the appropriate type of agency based on the incident category. In
this hackathon MVP, no real government agency is connected — every
submission goes to a demo backend that simulates what a real integration
would look like (assigning a tracking ID, storing the report, and marking a
status). The categories below are illustrative routing rules, not evidence
that any specific agency has agreed to receive reports through this app.

Typical routing (configurable, see config/settings.py -> AUTHORITIES):
- Theft, Robbery, Burglary, Assault, Vandalism, Domestic Incident, Property
  Damage, and general "Other" incidents are generally handled by a local
  police department's non-emergency reporting channel.
- Cybercrime, Fraud, Harassment, and Threats are often handled by a dedicated
  cybercrime or fraud reporting unit, in addition to or instead of local
  police, depending on jurisdiction.
- Missing person reports are handled by a missing persons unit and, in many
  jurisdictions, benefit from immediate reporting rather than delay.

Before connecting a real authority API in a production deployment, confirm
the agency's actual accepted submission format, authentication requirements,
and legal requirements for report handling in that jurisdiction.
