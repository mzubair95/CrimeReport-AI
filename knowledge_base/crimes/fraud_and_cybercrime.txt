Fraud involves deception for financial or personal gain, such as scams,
identity theft, fake investment schemes, or unauthorized use of payment
information. A useful fraud report includes: how the contact/deception
occurred (phone, email, in person, a fake website), what was requested or
taken (money, personal information, payment details), the amount involved if
known, and any account or transaction identifiers the victim already has
(without needing to disclose full card or account numbers within this app).

Cybercrime includes hacking, unauthorized account access, ransomware,
phishing, online impersonation, and other crimes carried out through computer
systems or the internet. Useful details include which account/service was
affected, when unusual activity was first noticed, whether any suspicious
links or attachments were opened, and whether the same password is reused
elsewhere (which should be changed as a precaution regardless of reporting
status).

Vandalism and property damage refer to the intentional damaging or destruction
of property that does not belong to the person responsible, such as graffiti,
broken windows, or damaged vehicles. Useful details include a description of
the damage, an approximate cost/extent, and whether the responsible party is
known or was seen.

Missing person reports involve a person whose whereabouts are unknown and
who is believed to be at risk. Time-sensitive details matter most: when and
where the person was last seen, what they were wearing, any known risk
factors (medical condition, age, prior history), and recent
communication. In many jurisdictions there is no mandatory waiting period to
report a missing person, especially a minor or a vulnerable adult — reporting
promptly is generally recommended rather than waiting.
